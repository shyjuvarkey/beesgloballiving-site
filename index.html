About us

Direct from the mill. Verified before it leaves the port.

Bees Global Living is the official Australian distributor of Hibernation Global Living, importing OEKO-TEX® certified 100% cotton hospitality towels and bed linen direct from India.

We work with hotels, resorts, spas and short-stay operators who need consistent, hotel-grade cotton at volume — without the mark-ups of a middleman. Every batch we bring in is tested and certified before it reaches Australian shores, so what your guests feel is exactly what was promised: soft, absorbent, durable, and safe on skin.

Every piece we distribute carries the same mill-to-market promise Hibernation was built on — sustainable sourcing, consistent quality, and reliable supply, now backed by local support and stock on the ground in Australia.
